ABOUT US

One Company, Every Building Solution

Sheikh Constructions is a Khulna-based construction company working across Khulna and Dhaka.

Our main business is construction and repairing work. With our KCC contractor licence, we take part in government tenders through e-GP and complete those works with full responsibility. We also provide interior designing, run supply works under our own trade licence, buy and sell flats in Dhaka and Khulna, sell flats and land as project partners with other companies, and carry out development work as a developer.
